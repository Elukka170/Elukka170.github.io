How do I win?

Get your cash down to (almost) zero before month 24 ends. Each act has a burn goal; hitting it on time earns a better grade. Watch the heat meter: too much flashy spending and the regulators shut you down.

Why can't I buy something?

  • Bank limit: your bank wires only so much a month. Wait a few seconds for it to refill.
  • No space: yachts need berths and crews, jets need hangars, cars need garages. Buy those first.
  • Too late: things that can't arrive before the last month ends are stamped TOO LATE.
  • Frozen: at high heat, regulators freeze some categories until it cools down.

Premium and restoring it

Premium is a one-time purchase that removes all ads and unlocks accessories for your billionaire. The game is exactly the same either way. On a new phone, open Options and tap Restore purchases while signed in with the same Apple ID.

Ads and tracking

The free version shows an ad between acts. You can change tracking any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Details are in the privacy policy.
