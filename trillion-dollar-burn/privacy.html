TRILLION DOLLAR BURN

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

The short version

  • The game has no accounts and never asks for your name, email or anything else about you.
  • Your runs, scores, settings and outfits stay on your phone.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may use your device's advertising identifier, but only if you allow tracking when iOS asks.
  • If you buy Premium, RevenueCat and Apple handle the purchase. We never see your payment details.

Who we are

Trillion Dollar Burn ("the game", "we") is an iPhone game made by an independent developer. Questions about this policy: harri_aho@hotmail.com.

What stays on your device

Your run in progress, finished runs and best scores, sound settings, and your billionaire's accessories are saved only on your device. We don't have a server and we can't see them. Deleting the game deletes them.

Ads (free version)

The free version shows full-screen ads between acts, served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect:

  • your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you tap Allow on the iOS tracking prompt;
  • device information such as model, operating system version and language;
  • an approximate location derived from your IP address;
  • ad interactions, such as which ads were shown and tapped;
  • diagnostic data such as crash and performance information.

If you're in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent (through Google's consent form) before any personalized ads are shown. You can decline, and the game plays exactly the same.

How Google uses this data: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services, and the Google Privacy Policy.

Premium purchase

Premium is a one-time in-app purchase that removes ads and unlocks accessories. Apple processes the payment. We use RevenueCat to check whether you own Premium. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase history for this game, not your name or payment details. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • Tracking: change it any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Personalized ads (EEA, UK, Switzerland): you're asked before any are shown; declining changes nothing in the game.
  • No ads at all: Premium removes them.
  • Deleting your data: deleting the game removes everything stored on your device. For data held by Google or RevenueCat, contact us and we'll help with your request.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
